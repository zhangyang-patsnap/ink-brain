# InkBrain visual direction contract

## Direction

Editorial Technical Atlas — a personal technical publication composed like a contemporary research folio: cool paper, near-black ink, vermilion editorial marks, exact metadata, and a navigable field of relationships.

## Product mechanism

InkBrain lets readers move between explanation, connected concepts, framework tracks, and tools while always seeing the evidence maturity of what they are reading.

## Audience scene

A developer arrives between focused work sessions, scans the author's technical territory, opens one promising thread, and follows explicit relationships rather than consuming an undifferentiated chronological feed.

## Cultural home

Independent technical journals, architectural atlases, research notebooks, API references, and the marginal annotation systems used to turn dense material into navigable knowledge.

## First viewport

The homepage opens as an editorial spread rather than a conventional hero. A compact navigation rail and publication identity frame a large thesis, while the existing ink-and-network artwork crosses the content grid and resolves into four destinations. A visible maturity legend proves that the site distinguishes ideas from verified work before the visitor scrolls.

## Visitor path

Orient through the thesis → choose one of four technical territories → inspect a current thread → follow related identifiers into another content type.

## Signature interaction

On capable pointers, a restrained vermilion route line travels between the four territories as focus moves. Keyboard focus produces the same relationship cue. Reduced-motion keeps the complete route visible and static.

## Cross-surface reach

- Reading pages become quiet journal columns with marginal metadata.
- Knowledge pages become an indexed atlas with explicit relationship lines.
- Framework pages use comparison registers and evidence-state labels.
- Tool pages become precise work surfaces without adopting dashboard chrome.

## Raised disciplines

- Data-sublime discipline: metadata aligns to strict tabular rhythm and never becomes decoration.
- Teletext discipline: every destination has a stable, visible address.
- Layered-paper discipline: hierarchy comes from overlap and flat fields, not ornamental shadows.
- Automata discipline: motion reveals relationships and stops completely under reduced motion.
- Instruction-manual discipline: status and next action remain legible without explanatory marketing copy.

## Honest risk

Editorial asymmetry can become precious or slow to scan. The implementation must keep primary routes obvious, reading measures conventional, and mobile order strictly linear.

## Quality bar

The result should feel authored at the level of a small independent technical journal: decisive composition, production typography, real content rhythm, and no generic card-grid scaffolding.
