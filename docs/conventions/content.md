# Content conventions

Articles answer a complete engineering question. Knowledge entries define one reusable concept and connect it to articles, frameworks, and neighboring concepts.

Every article should cover, where applicable:

1. Concept and boundary
2. Value and trade-offs
3. Usage and a reproducible example
4. Runtime principle or source call chain
5. Failure behavior and recovery
6. Practical judgment and applicable limits

Maturity values:

- `idea`: hypothesis or design direction
- `studied`: source and documentation review completed
- `implemented`: working implementation exists
- `verified`: repeatable test or runtime evidence exists
- `production`: backed by production evidence

Do not promote maturity because an article is polished. Content status and engineering evidence are separate.
